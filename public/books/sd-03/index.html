Case file SD-03

The Year of the Dog

Book 3 of 8 in the Sleeping Dogs series  ·  First published 2016  ·  Formats vary by retailer

Terror cells lie embedded across the American homeland, waiting on a signal — and the strike they’re waiting for is designed to leave the nation defenseless. Abroad, China consolidates its dominion across Asia while Russia menaces Europe; at home, the people who should be stopping it are compromised, complacent, or worse.

Brendan Whelan has one option: pull the quarreling, scattered Dogs back into the field and unleash the Hounds of Hell before the clock runs out. Getting them to work together again may be harder than the mission itself.
