Hidden Dragons,
Sleeping Dogs

The ninth Sleeping Dogs thriller is finished and in editing, with release set for November 2026. Request newsletter updates for the confirmed on-sale date.

Read Sample Chapter → Get release updates

Nature made the Sleeping Dogs the slow way — by evolution. Beijing has decided to skip the wait. Using CRISPR gene-editing technology, the Chinese Communist Party has built super-soldiers of its own and quietly moved them inside the United States. The engineered against the born. The lab-made against nature-made.

The Dogs were called Homo maximus by the scientists who found them — beta models of the species that follows ours: stronger, faster, smarter. In Hidden Dragons, the threat is deliberately engineered: CRISPR-built adversaries against the naturally evolved Dogs.

Frequently asked

When does it come out?

November 2026. The manuscript is finished and in editing now; the exact on-sale date and final cover reveal will be shared here and in the newsletter. You can read Chapter One now.

Do I need to read the other eight first?

Each book stands alone, but the series rewards reading in order. If you're new, start with The Awakening — the ebook starts at US$0.99. Prices and availability vary by retailer and country.

Will there be a book ten?

Yes. Hidden Dragons is the ninth Sleeping Dogs thriller, not the last one — more are coming. In the author's words: we haven't heard the last from those Dogs.
